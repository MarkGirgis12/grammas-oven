Our bakery

Old-world recipes, made fresh every day

Gramma's Oven is a European bakery and café on Yonge Street. Stop in for a loaf to take home, a box of pastries for the office, or sit down with a sandwich and a cappuccino.

🍞

Home-made breads

Raisin, cinnamon raisin, light rye, walnut sourdough and crusty French baguettes.

🥐

Pastries & cakes

Croissants, chocolate croissants, apple and poppyseed Danishes, cookies and cakes.

☕

Café & lunch

Panini, turkey and roast beef sandwiches, soups, and coffee to stay or to go.
